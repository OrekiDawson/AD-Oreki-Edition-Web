## Eternity + Eternity Challenges

The following Google Spreadsheet is broken down into several pages and spans all of Eternity to Dilation: [https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1NrYADsW4s7wRYTE91Z0EFHbXcHaswuuMzG9a2WyGG0A/edit#gid=244460140](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1NrYADsW4s7wRYTE91Z0EFHbXcHaswuuMzG9a2WyGG0A/edit#gid=244460140)

Pages:
Eternity Start - A step by step of what to do and buy all the way to Eternity Challenge 1.
EC FAQ - A breakdown of each Eternity Challenge, their requirements and recommended ways of tackling them.
Picturemap v2.5 - A full list of all Eternity Challenges with their TT and Tree recommendations.
EC Planner v2.5 - The list of Eternity Challenges with a bit more information and partial steps in between.
EC Order - Just the recommended order of challenges so you can skip the previous pages and try them out on your own.
